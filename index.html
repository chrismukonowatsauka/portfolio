Supplier Reconciliation Tool

Jan 2026

GAIN Cash and Carry · built as an Accounts Clerk

Paste a supplier statement and the ledger, and get a full reconciliation that explains every difference.

  • Accounts payable
  • Supplier reconciliations
  • Excel
  • JavaScript
Open tool
Case study

Problem

I was responsible for keeping 40+ supplier accounts accurate. Matching each statement to the Openbravo ledger line by line was slow, and errors such as duplicated invoices or wrong invoice numbers were easy to miss.

What I built

A reconciliation template in Excel, later rebuilt as a browser tool. The user pastes two data sets, the supplier statement and the ledger, and the tool produces the reconciliation.

Checks it runs

  • Duplicate entries and invoices posted twice
  • Wrong or transposed invoice numbers
  • Overstated and understated invoices
  • Payments and credit notes matched to the statement, with unmatched payments listed
  • Genuine partial receipts (goods received over two days) recognised, so they are not mistaken for double postings

Outcome

Differences are listed with a reason, so follow-up with suppliers starts from a clear list instead of a manual search.

Sales Reconciliation Workbook

Jul 2026

GAIN Cash and Carry · internal workbook

Reconciles Openbravo sales exports to branch cash-up reports by currency (USD, ZiG, ZAR) and Nostro bank.

  • Sales and cash reconciliations
  • ZiG / USD / ZAR
  • Excel
Internal workbook. Demo version available on request.
Case study

Problem

I reconcile daily sales and banking for 8 branches (about US$2.6M in monthly deposits) across three currencies. Each branch's figures had to be matched by hand from the system export and the cashier's reports.

What I built

A paste-in workbook: paste the Openbravo sales export and the branch cash-up report, and live formulas produce the reconciliation.

How it works

  • Splits sales by currency and by Nostro bank
  • Calculates reconciling items such as card swipes, transfers and manual invoices
  • Leaves one daily variance per currency to investigate

Outcome

Investigation starts from a single variance per currency per day, and the formulas stay live so every figure can be traced.
